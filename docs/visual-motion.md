The original circular hero is the default and shows the generated white plate of rigatoni all’amatriciana (`/images/rigatoni-feature.webp`). The alternate cinematic hero keeps its carbonara photograph. A generated amatriciana rigatoni cutout at `/images/rigatoni-piece.webp` follows the scroll from the classic hero plate, through the matching pasta category, into the amatriciana feature.
