## Historical and parking leads

[Accademia Italiana della Cucina, Civiltà della Tavola, February 2026](https://accademiaitalianadellacucina.it/sites/default/files/allegati/CDT_389_feb_2026_ITA_0.pdf) contains a firsthand dining-event report dated 16 December 2025. Its indexed text corroborates the address and telephone, describes family management, a private restaurant car park and Monday closure. It also discusses Giovanni Grappasonni, the earlier name “Trattoria Uva di Roma da Grappasonni”, four generations and a 2015 renewal. These are research leads, not confirmation from the restaurant. The publication's indexed text is inconsistent about the son's surname (Grani / Girani), so no owner names or family timeline should be published from this evidence alone. The full PDF exceeded the browsing tool's document-size limit.

- A public Facebook page title verifies that the page exists under that identity; it does not prove every restaurant fact or establish photographic reuse rights.
- Keep generated image captions visible and describe the assets as illustrative placeholders, not actual photographs of Grappasonni.
