## Image captions

The restaurant-gallery photographs provide authentic location and food imagery. Exact dish names cannot be established from those photographs alone. Do not label `dish-01` to `dish-04` as any particular menu item. A generic “Dalla nostra cucina” or “I dolci della casa” caption is appropriate.
